# PRODUCT.md — Avolab Solutions (avolab.ca)

## What this is

Marketing + showcase site for Avolab Solutions, a Canadian tech studio. Two audiences at once:

1. **Clients** (AI automation, workflow/data plumbing, product engineering) — decision-makers who want proof, not slogans.
2. **Product visitors** — people arriving for RepFinder, Snapkeep, DashVerse et al., and players landing via the Arcade.

## Product truth

- The studio ships and maintains its own products: **12 live** (source of truth: `lib/apps.ts` — DashVerse, RepFinder, Snapkeep, Pitch Therapy, GANGSIGN.GG, SignFlow, Newsltr, Rent Central, Friday Evacuation, BREAKRUN, Soundspace, Print Shop Sim).
- Of those: **5** are games/sims, **2** are privacy-first on-device tools (Snapkeep, SignFlow), **1** is playable embedded on this page (DashVerse).
- Client services are the same machinery used on the shelf: AI agents & automation, product engineering, on-device computer vision, games/interactive, privacy-first engineering, data/workflow plumbing.
- Contact: hello@avolab.ca. Newsletter: **Ship Notes** (monthly digest). Monetized with AdSense (afterInteractive).

## Claims discipline

Never invent client names, metrics, or testimonials. Every number on the page must be derivable from `lib/apps.ts` or the public repos. No affiliation claims with other brands.

## Voice

Direct, dry, confident. Short sentences. Canadian spelling. Banned words: "empowering", "seamless", "solutions" (as filler), "revolutionary". Evidence over adjectives: prefer a named product over an abstract claim.

## Success criteria

A visitor can, within seconds: know what the studio does, name 2–3 real products, play one product without leaving the page, and reach hello@avolab.ca. On mobile: silky scroll, ≥44px targets, no horizontal overflow.
